= Monitor scanning health in AI Data Engine
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before you act on a dashboard or investigation finding, confirm that the underlying data is current. Use this topic to verify that scanning is working and that your findings reflect up-to-date data.

== Verify scan status and coverage

Confirm that all your storage systems are connected and being scanned. The steps differ depending on your deployment type.

[role="tabbed-block"]
====
.Lite
--
On a single-node (Lite) deployment, confirm that all your storage systems are connected and being scanned.

.Steps
. From the AI Data Engine UI, click *Configuration*.
. On the Scanned systems tab, confirm that every storage system you expect to see is listed and enabled for scanning.
. Click a system row to review its scan progress and any errors. See link:../data-sources/manage-data-sources.html[Manage data sources] for the full detail view.
--

.Enterprise
--
On a multi-node (Enterprise) deployment, scan health is tracked per node in addition to per system.

.Steps
. From the AI Data Engine UI, click *Configuration*.
. Review the total node count and the status of each node.
. Identify which node, if any, is reporting errors or falling behind on scan coverage, and address that node before trusting findings that depend on it.

== Validate that investigation findings reflect current data

If the dashboard shows a large amount of cold or stale data, confirm the number is current before you act on it.

* Check the last-scanned time for each storage system contributing to the finding. A system that hasn't scanned recently may under- or over-report reclaimable storage.
* Confirm scan coverage is complete for that system. A partial scan (paused, in progress, or with errors) means some data hasn't been evaluated yet.
* If coverage looks incomplete, wait for the next scan cycle or resolve the underlying error before acting on the finding.

== Dark site considerations

Scanning health monitoring works the same way in air-gapped (dark site) deployments as in connected deployments, with these differences:

* No telemetry is sent externally. Health monitoring is fully local to your deployment.
* Software and content updates are installed manually as a quarterly bundle, rather than continuously, so scan behavior may lag behind the latest release until the next bundle is applied.
