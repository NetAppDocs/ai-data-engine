= Create a policy in AI Data Engine
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Create a policy when you want to monitor a recurring data condition, such as files going cold or growing beyond a size threshold, without repeating the same investigation manually. For example, a data governance manager can save a "large stale files" policy to track that grouping across scan cycles and receive an email update when the impacted file count changes.

== About policies

Policies save the filter criteria you define in Investigation as a reusable rule. AI Data Engine evaluates each policy against the metadata catalog after every scan cycle. You can open a policy at any time to view the current matching result set, or attach an action — such as a deletion or email notification — that runs automatically when the policy criteria are met.

== Choose a policy action

A policy is a saved filter set with an optional action attached:

* *Monitor only (no action)*: Track the matching grouping over time without acting. Use this when you want visibility before you automate a response.
* *Send email notification*: Notify a user by email when the matching file count changes.
* *Delete permanently*: AI Data Engine automatically applies the action to files that match the policy criteria.

== Create a policy from Investigation

.Before you begin
* You must have the _AI Data Engine administrator_ role.

.Steps
. In NetApp Console, navigate to *Governance* > *AI Data Engine* > *Investigation*.
. Apply one or more filters that identify the files you want the policy to target.
+
For example: *File Type* = .vmdk AND *Last Accessed Time* > 365 days.
+
Review the results table to confirm the filter set captures the intended data grouping.

. In the filter action bar, click *Create policy*.

. Enter a *Policy name*.

. Optionally, enter a *Description* (up to 500 characters).

. The filter criteria are captured as a query expression (for example, `last_access_time <= 3 Years AND file_size >= 5GiB`).

. Optionally, select an *Action* and choose the notification type (for example, *Send email notification*).

. Click *Create*.

.Result
The policy is saved and appears on the link:manage-policies.html[Policies] page. If you associated an action, AI Data Engine applies that action to files that match the policy criteria.

== Example: identify cold archive files

The following example creates a policy to send an email notification when files that have not been accessed in over a year are detected.

. In Investigation, apply these filters:
+
* *Last Accessed Time* > 365 days

. Click *Create policy*.
. Enter the policy name: *Cold Files — Unaccessed 1 Year*.
. Select the action *Send email notification* and enter the recipient email address.
. Click *Create*.
