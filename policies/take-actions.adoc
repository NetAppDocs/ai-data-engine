== Tag

// RESEARCH NEEDED (LOW): Confirm where business tags are defined and managed — is there a tag management page, or are tags entered ad hoc at action time? Confirm whether tags applied by AI Data Engine are visible in ONTAP or other NetApp products.

Tagging applies a business tag to files. Tags are user-defined metadata labels for classifying data according to your organization's taxonomy. You can tag files manually from the Investigation view or automatically through a policy.

.Steps
. In NetApp Console, navigate to *Governance* > *AI Data Engine* > *Investigation*.
. Apply filters to identify the files you want to tag.
. In the action bar, click *Tag*.
. Select or enter the tag value.
. Confirm the action.

NOTE: To tag files automatically, link:create-policy.html[create a policy] with the *Tag* action selected.

== Archive

// RESEARCH NEEDED (LOW): Confirm the archiving destination for this release — ONTAP FabricPool, object store, or another target. This determines how the action should be described to customers.

Archiving moves files to a cold storage destination. You can archive files manually from the Investigation view or automatically through a policy.

WARNING: Confirm your intended archive destination before taking this action.

.Before you begin
* You must have the Storage Ops role or higher.

.Steps
. In NetApp Console, navigate to *Governance* > *AI Data Engine* > *Investigation*.
. Apply filters to identify the files you want to archive.
. In the action bar, click *Archive*.
. Confirm the action.

// RESEARCH NEEDED (LOW): Confirm the post-archive state — does the file remain visible in Investigation with a status change, or does it disappear from results?

NOTE: To archive files automatically, link:create-policy.html[create a policy] with the *Archive* action selected.

== Delete

Deleting permanently removes files from storage. You can delete files manually from the Investigation view or automatically through a policy.

WARNING: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

.Before you begin
* You must have the Storage Ops role or higher.

.Steps
. In NetApp Console, navigate to *Governance* > *AI Data Engine* > *Investigation*.
. Apply filters to identify the files you want to delete.
. In the action bar, click *Delete*.
. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

.Result
The selected files are permanently deleted from storage.

NOTE: To delete files automatically, link:create-policy.html[create a policy] with the *Delete* action selected.
