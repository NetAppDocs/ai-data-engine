= Learn about AI Data Engine
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
AI Data Engine is a metadata intelligence service that gives you visibility into structured and unstructured data stored on your storage systems. By scanning your storage systems and cataloging file system metadata, AI Data Engine helps you identify storage optimization opportunities, investigate your data, and enforce governance policies by analyzing metadata in place, without moving or copying data.

== What AI Data Engine does

AI Data Engine scans connected storage systems to extract file system metadata and builds a catalog that is updated after each scan cycle. From this catalog, you can:

* View storage optimization insights on a dashboard, including which data owners hold potentially reclaimable storage, including files meeting your organization's cold and stale thresholds, and duplicate files
* Investigate your data using flexible metadata filters to build targeted data grouping
* Create policies that save investigation criteria as reusable rules
* Take actions on data: tagging, archiving, copying, or deleting files that match policy criteria

== Storage optimization and data governance

AI Data Engine focuses on storage optimization and data lifecycle management for structured and unstructured data stored on your storage systems.

== Who uses AI Data Engine

AI Data Engine is designed for the following users:

* *Storage administrators* who need to identify and monitor which data owners hold files meeting their organization's cold and stale threshold, identify duplicate files, and evaluate storage optimization opportunities.
* *IT operations teams* who need a central view of what structured and unstructured data exists across their storage environment.

== Where AI Data Engine lives

You access AI Data Engine through NetApp Console, under *Governance > AI Data Engine*. You deploy it as a software instance on Linux infrastructure you manage, either a virtual machine in your cloud account or a machine in your on-premises environment. Two editions are available: *Lite* for single-node deployments and *Enterprise* for multi-node Kubernetes clusters.

AI Data Engine scans data on NetApp ONTAP and StorageGRID systems, as well as on non-NetApp systems that support the NFS, SMB/CIFS, or S3 protocols.
