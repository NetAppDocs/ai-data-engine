= Monitor scanning health in AI Data Engine
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before you act on a dashboard or investigation finding, confirm that the underlying data is current. Verify that scanning is working and that your findings reflect up-to-date data.

== Verify scan status and coverage

Confirm that all your storage systems are connected and being scanned.

.Steps
. From the AI Data Engine UI, select *Configuration*.
. On the *Scanned systems* tab, confirm that every storage system you expect to see is listed and enabled for scanning.
. Select *View details* from a system row to review scan progress and any errors. Refer to link:../data-sources/manage-data-sources.html[Manage data sources] for details.

== Validate that investigation findings reflect current data

If the dashboard shows a large amount of cold or stale data, confirm the numbers are current before making decisions based on them.

* Check the last-scanned time for each storage system contributing to the finding. A system that hasn't scanned recently might under- or over-report cold and stale data.
* Confirm scan coverage is complete for that system. A partial scan (paused, in progress, or with errors) means some data hasn't been evaluated yet.
* Wait for the next scan cycle or resolve the underlying error if coverage looks incomplete.
